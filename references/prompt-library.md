# Prompt Library

Use these as building blocks. Adapt composition, color, density, platform, and product meaning; never paste every block together unchanged.

## Frosted Glass

### Chinese

使用现代、克制的磨玻璃视觉风格。将需要表达上下文或信息层级的内容放置在半透明面板中，加入柔和的背景模糊、低对比度细边框和局部高光。通过不同透明度建立前后关系，让背景色彩能够隐约透过面板。整体干净、轻盈、专业，避免过强阴影、塑料质感、乳白色遮罩和夸张的玻璃反光。

### English

Use a modern and restrained frosted-glass language for contextual and layered information. Place selected content inside translucent panels with soft background blur, low-contrast hairline borders, and localized highlights. Establish depth through controlled transparency while allowing background color to softly pass through. Keep the interface clean, lightweight, and professional. Avoid heavy shadows, milky overlays, plastic surfaces, and exaggerated reflections.

## Diffused Gradient with Grain

### Chinese

使用柔和、弥散的多色渐变表达信息的流动与生成，让有限的品牌色在大尺度空间中自然过渡。叠加低透明度的细腻胶片颗粒和数字噪点，增加材质与信息密度。渐变应当像环境场和状态变化，而不是普通的线性装饰。保留足够中性色与留白，避免默认蓝紫渐变、全画面高饱和以及粗糙噪点。

### English

Use a soft, diffused color field to express information flow and generation, allowing a limited brand palette to transition naturally across large spatial areas. Add low-opacity fine film grain and digital noise for material presence and information density. The gradient should behave like an ambient field or changing state rather than a decorative linear gradient. Preserve neutral space and avoid default blue-purple blends, full-canvas saturation, and coarse noise.

## Technical Grid and Form-like Layout

### Chinese

使用结构化、表单式的界面语言，以纤细直线、规则网格和清晰模块组织内容。加入小型字段标签、状态标记、坐标信息或参数式元数据，并克制地使用等宽字体。参考专业数据工具和开发者控制台，同时保持充足留白与编辑设计感。线条应轻盈精确，避免密集表格、厚重边框和传统企业后台的压迫感。

### English

Use a structured, form-like interface organized through precise hairline dividers, a consistent grid, and clearly defined modules. Introduce small field labels, status tags, coordinates, or parameter-style metadata with restrained monospaced typography. Reference professional data tools and developer consoles while preserving generous whitespace and an editorial feel. Avoid dense tables, heavy borders, and conventional enterprise-dashboard weight.

## Ambient Light and Subtle Glow

### Chinese

使用柔和、具有方向性的环境光表达系统的激活、感知或生成状态。让光线仅从关键节点、界面边缘或图层之间自然渗透，形成克制的光晕、局部高光与轻微柔焦。光必须承担状态和视觉焦点，而不是成为装饰。保持通透与空气感，避免赛博朋克、高饱和霓虹、通篇描边发光和过度 bloom。

### English

Use soft, directional ambient light to signal activation, perception, or generation. Allow light to emerge only from key nodes, interface edges, or between selected layers, producing restrained halos, localized highlights, and subtle bloom. Make light carry state and hierarchy rather than decoration. Keep the result luminous and spacious; avoid cyberpunk styling, saturated neon, universal glowing outlines, and excessive bloom.

## Blurred Abstract Forms

### Chinese

在背景或品牌区加入大尺度、低细节的抽象形体，用圆润但不完全规则的轮廓、柔和景深、局部运动模糊与半透明叠色，表达数据、光与物质之间的中间状态。形体只负责建立氛围和空间层次，不得争夺主要内容。避免常见的随机 3D 球体、液态金属和无意义漂浮物。

### English

Introduce large-scale, low-detail abstract forms in brand or background areas. Use rounded yet imperfect silhouettes, soft depth of field, localized motion blur, and translucent color overlays to suggest a state between data, light, and matter. Use the forms to create atmosphere and spatial depth without competing with primary content. Avoid generic 3D spheres, liquid metal, and arbitrary floating objects.

## Master prompt structure

Build the final prompt in this order:

1. Product and target surface
2. AI behavior and visual metaphor
3. Composition and information hierarchy
4. Selected Soft Tech recipe and the role of each ingredient
5. Color and typography
6. Material, light, texture, and motion intensity
7. Usability and implementation constraints
8. Negative direction
