# Product Reference Notes

Use these as analytical references, not templates to imitate. Verify live sites when current visuals matter.

## Cohere

Observe how enterprise AI can communicate control, trust, security, and product breadth without depending on magical AI imagery. Useful for mature hierarchy, product screenshots, and business credibility.

## TwelveLabs

Observe how gradients, grain, abstract forms, charts, and technical labels can express multimodal perception and media intelligence. Useful for balancing sensing with structured analysis.

## AssemblyAI

Observe technical grids, form structures, hairline borders, code, parameters, and real-time data. Useful for turning an abstract model capability into a configurable professional system.

## Genie

Observe how a personal AI product can use emotion, typography, color, motion, and brand world-building instead of developer-tool conventions. Useful for consumer, companion, and lifestyle products.

## Inspiration feeds

- Cosmos: collect atmosphere, color, typography, material, light, and motion.
- Variant: explore alternative design directions and identify unnamed preferences.
- Mobbin: study product patterns, interaction states, onboarding, generation, feedback, error, and empty states.

Working shorthand: `Cosmos for atmosphere, Variant for possibilities, Mobbin for product solutions.`
